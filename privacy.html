Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains what the Moni desktop application ("Moni") does with data. Moni runs on your own computer; we do not operate a server that stores your videos or account data.

What stays on your device

Settings, API keys, generated scripts, scenes, voiceovers and finished videos are stored locally on your computer. We do not receive them.

TikTok

If you choose to connect TikTok, Moni asks TikTok for your permission through TikTok's official login. Moni receives an access token and basic profile information (such as your display name) and uses them only to upload videos you choose to post. The token is stored locally on your computer. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok account settings under apps and permissions, or by disconnecting in Moni. Moni does not read your TikTok messages, followers, or other private data.

Third-party services you configure

To generate content, Moni sends the text prompts and media you choose to the AI, voice, and video services you set up (for example a language model, a text-to-speech service, or a GPU notebook you run yourself). Those services process data under their own privacy policies.

Sharing, retention and children

We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not collect it. Data on your device stays until you delete it. Moni is not directed at children under 13.
